Kelvin-Helmholtz Wave Simulator

The Richardson Number (Ri) is a dimensionless ratio of buoyant suppression of turbulence to shear generation of turbulence.
It can be defined as:
$$\mathbfit{Ri} = \frac{\mathbfit{N}^2}{\mathbfit{S}^2} = \frac{\mathbfit{N}^2}{(\boldsymbol{\Delta} \mathbfit{u}/\boldsymbol{\Delta} \mathbfit{z})^2}$$

Instability between fluid layers occurs when Ri < 0.25
